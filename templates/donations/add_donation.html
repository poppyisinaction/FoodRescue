Food Safety Protocols
  • Hot Foods: Must be maintained above 60°C until handed over or properly cooled within 2 hours.
  • Chilled Items: Keep dairy, meats, and prepared salads refrigerated at 4°C or below.
  • Airtight Packaging: Pack food into clean food-grade foil, containers, or sealed boxes.
  • No Spoiled Food: Never donate food that has been exposed to unhygienic conditions or shows discoloration.
