How FoodRescue Works

A seamless 4-step technological bridge connecting surplus food with communities in need.

1. List Surplus

Restaurants, caterers, or event organizers upload surplus food details, quantity, photos, and expiry windows in under 60 seconds.

2. Real-Time Alert

Nearby registered and verified NGOs instantly receive notifications about newly available meals within their distribution zones.

3. Claim & Pickup

The NGO submits a claim. The donor accepts, securely reserving the food and coordinating the exact pickup window.

4. Zero Waste Impact

Food is collected, safely inspected, distributed to shelters, and digitally recorded in our auditable donation history ledger.

FoodRescue AI: Cooked meals should ideally be chilled within 2 hours of preparation and consumed within 4-6 hours if unrefrigerated. Hot foods must stay above 60°C, while chilled items must remain at 4°C or below.

Why Join FoodRescue?

Transforming commercial waste into community nourishment and environmental stewardship.

For Food Donors
  • Eliminate disposal logistics and landfill tipping costs.
  • Enhance corporate social responsibility (CSR) ratings.
  • Receive automated digital donation records for audit compliance.
  • Foster goodwill and community pride among staff.
For NGOs & Charities
  • Access fresh, high-quality nutrition for free.
  • Real-time notifications eliminate missed opportunities.
  • Strict temperature and expiry tracking protects beneficiaries.
  • Transparent race-condition protection ensures fair allocation.
For Our Planet
  • Mitigates dangerous methane emissions from rotting landfills.
  • Saves thousands of liters of embedded agricultural water.
  • Directly supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 12.3 & Goal 2.
  • Fosters circular community economies across cities.
